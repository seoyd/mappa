# OpenStreetMap-derived map data

`korea_osm_roads.pmtiles` and `seoul_osm_streets.pmtiles` contain data derived from OpenStreetMap contributors, obtained as the dated South Korea extract from Geofabrik. These two databases are made available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/). Any rights in individual contents of the databases are licensed under the [Database Contents License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1.0/).

The Rust source code and the Natural Earth-only assets have their own terms. Their inclusion alongside these databases does not change the OSM-derived databases' license.
